# Idea Lab
Find a genuine conceptual hook rather than a list of generic advertising headlines. Read project details, look for striking contrasts, overlooked objects, unusual constraints, consequences and human decisions. Generate directions based on a concrete detail, a reversal of perspective, a scene, a central question, and an unexpected but valid connection. Test each for specificity, relevance, defensibility and whether a competitor could say it unchanged. Choose one strong direction; do not turn every section into a slogan. Imagination is welcome for metaphor and treatment, never for unverified claims.

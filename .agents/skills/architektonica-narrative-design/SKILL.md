# Narrative Design
Give each section a narrative job: attract interest, build understanding, reveal distinctive proof, answer doubt or invite action. Use scenes and project details when they illuminate the audience's decision. Avoid mechanically repeating problem-solution-benefits on every page. Work from available assets and the real project timeline; do not invent production steps. Design rhythm through contrast of short and long passages. The structure should help a visitor understand why Architektonica's work matters and what to do next.

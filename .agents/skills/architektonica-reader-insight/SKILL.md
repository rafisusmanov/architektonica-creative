# Reader Insight
Build reader understanding from real source material and customer research. Distinguish a role from a motive: a developer, marketing lead or R&D scientist can have different decision criteria. Identify the visitor's triggering situation, job-to-be-done, desired decision, anxieties, vocabulary and proof requirements. Do not fabricate interviews or claim hypothetical motivations are established audience facts. Translate findings into useful copy angles and proof placement.

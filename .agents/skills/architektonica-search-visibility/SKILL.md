# Search Visibility
Respect search intent, descriptive information hierarchy, accurate entity naming and factual specificity. Produce SEO title, meta description, purposeful headings, FAQs and internal-link ideas when relevant. Keep brands, named projects and technical capabilities consistent with sources. Write concise answerable paragraphs where useful for AI retrieval, but preserve the page's authorial narrative. Never claim a guaranteed ranking or AI citation. Verify keyword volumes or current search behavior rather than inventing them.
